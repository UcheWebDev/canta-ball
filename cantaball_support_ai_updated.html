HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap one of your players, then tap the pitch to move (they turn to face where they run).
  • The ball carrier can pass to a teammate or shoot when in range.
  • To shoot: pick LEFT / CENTER / RIGHT and check the aiming line, then press TAKE SHOT. The ball follows the line exactly, unless a defender is standing on it (the line turns red).
  • Win the ball by tackling the carrier from the front. Side or back tackles are fouls.
  • Before each match you get 1 minute to set up: choose the game duration and optionally select a ⭐ star player (shoots from anywhere), then press READY. The match starts when both players are ready.
  • Matches use the selected duration. First to 3 goals wins, or lead when time runs out.
  • Closed the tab by accident? Reopen the page and you'll rejoin automatically.
