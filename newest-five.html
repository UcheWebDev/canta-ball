HOW TO PLAY
  • Tap one of your players, then tap the pitch to move (they turn to face where they run).
  • The ball carrier can pass to a teammate or shoot when in range.
  • Win the ball by tackling the carrier from the front. Side or back tackles are fouls.
  • Matches last 5 minutes. First to 3 goals wins, or lead when time runs out.
